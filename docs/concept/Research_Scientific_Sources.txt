**Sleep Duration, Health Outcomes, and Hygiene**  
- Watson NF, Badr MS, Belenky G, et al. Recommended Amount of Sleep for a Healthy Adult: A Joint Consensus Statement of the American Academy of Sleep Medicine and Sleep Research Society. *Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine*. 2015;11(6):591–592. American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) / Sleep Research Society (SRS). [https://jcsm.aasm.org/doi/10.5664/jcsm.4758](https://jcsm.aasm.org/doi/10.5664/jcsm.4758) (also published in *SLEEP*, 2015).  
- National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI), National Institutes of Health (NIH). Sleep Deprivation and Deficiency: How Much Sleep Is Enough. Updated 2022. NIH/NHLBI. [https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/how-much-sleep](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/how-much-sleep).  
- Li J, Cao D, Huang Y, et al. Sleep duration and health outcomes: an umbrella review. *Sleep & Breathing*. 2022;26:1479–1492. (Peer-reviewed meta-analyses synthesis). [https://doi.org/10.1007/s11325-021-02458-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11325-021-02458-1).  
- American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM). Healthy Sleep Habits. Sleep Education by the AASM. Updated ~2021. AASM. [https://sleepeducation.org/healthy-sleep/healthy-sleep-habits/](https://sleepeducation.org/healthy-sleep/healthy-sleep-habits/).

**Nutrition (Protein and Intermittent Fasting / Time-Restricted Eating)**  
- Jäger R, Kerksick CM, Campbell BI, et al. International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: protein and exercise. *Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition*. 2017;14:20. International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN). [https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8) (PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5477153/).  
- Hua Y, et al. Intermittent fasting for weight management and metabolic health: An updated comprehensive umbrella review of health outcomes. *Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism*. 2025. (Umbrella review of meta-analyses). [https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.16092](https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.16092).  
- Related umbrella/network meta-analyses on intermittent fasting metabolic outcomes (e.g., *BMC Medicine*, 2024; *eClinicalMedicine*, 2024). Multiple peer-reviewed syntheses supporting time-restricted eating effects primarily in overweight/obese adults.  

**Meditation, Cold Exposure, and Related**  
- Systematic review on Vipassana meditation health impacts (psychological, physiological, neurobiological outcomes). Published ~2025 (PubMed-indexed synthesis of studies since 2010). (Moderate evidence for stress/anxiety reduction; intensity-dependent effects). [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41158896/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41158896/).  
- Systematic reviews/meta-analyses on cold-water immersion (mood, stress, recovery; e.g., Frontiers in Physiology 2026 and related). Evidence limited/heterogeneous for sustained psychological benefits; clearer for acute responses.  

**Wearables and Sleep Tracking Validation**  
- Svensson T, Madhawa K, Nt H, Chung U, Svensson AK. Validity and reliability of the Oura Ring Generation 3 (Gen3) with Oura sleep staging algorithm 2.0 (OSSA 2.0) when compared to multi-night ambulatory polysomnography: A validation study of 96 participants and 421,045 epochs. *Sleep Medicine*. 2024;115:251–263. (University-affiliated validation vs. PSG; Oura-related support noted). [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sleep.2024.01.020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sleep.2024.01.020) (PubMed: 38382312).  

These sources are ordered by primary topic relevance within each category and prioritize consensus guidelines, position stands, umbrella reviews, and direct validation studies from major medical/scientific organizations. Links point to free full-text or abstract pages where available.
